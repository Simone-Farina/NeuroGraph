## Design Context

### Users
Adult, lifelong learners (28-35 years old). Ambitious but overwhelmed by information abundance ("My brain's context window isn't 1 million tokens"). Emotional state: Explorer granted access to an immensely powerful, quiet tool. Should feel like a sanctuary from the noisy web. Introspective, calm, deeply focused. Mitigates learning difficulties and cognitive overload.

### Brand Personality
Introspective. Spatial. Tactile.
Vibe: A minimalist Danish notebook infused with raw computational power. Marries warm, elegant typography with surgical, grid-like precision.
References: Dimensional (tactile dark mode, typography), Obsidian/Notion (personal knowledge vault), TheSecondBrain.io (spatial canvas).

### Aesthetic Direction
Strictly Dark Theme. Anti-pattern: Generic AI Slop (no glowing cyan/purple gradients, no neon laser-beams).
Backgrounds: Charcoal Paper or Graphite. Off-blacks (#121212, #18181B) rather than pure black to reduce eye strain. Subtle, extremely low-opacity dot-grids on the React Flow canvas (the "computation" aspect).
Typography: 
- UI Elements (Menus, Sidebar, Buttons): Clean geometric sans-serif (Geist, Inter).
- The Knowledge (Neurons, Chat, Markdown): Beautiful, highly readable Serif (Newsreader, Merriweather) or an elegant Mono. Reading a Neuron should feel like reading a high-end editorial piece.
Spatial Feel: Tactile. Subtle 1px borders (border-white/10), very soft glassmorphic blurs for floating toolbars, elegant, slightly eased animations (Framer Motion).

### Design Principles (Semantic Color System)
Color must be used purely to communicate the state of the learning DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph), never just for decoration:
1. **The Baseline**: UI elements are monochrome (grays, whites, zincs).
2. **Mastered Neurons (Healthy)**: "Sage Green" or "Muted Forest" (emerald-600/80 to emerald-900). Represents calm, consolidated knowledge.
3. **Ghost Nodes (The Frontier)**: "Ghostly Silver" or "Architectural Slate". Rendered with dashed borders and partial opacity. Uncharted territory.
4. **Decaying Nodes (Soft-FIRe/FSRS expiration)**: "Burnt Terracotta" or "Oxidized Copper" (orange-500/80 or rust). Signals urgency ("rusting" knowledge) without screaming like a bright red error.
5. **Synaptic Activity (AI Thinking/Generation)**: "Bioluminescent White" or "Warm Amber". A subtle glow or pulse when the AI Bouncer is thinking or Neurogenesis is occurring.

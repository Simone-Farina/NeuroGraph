| Behavior | Requirement | Why Manual | Test Instructions |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Graph-side target setting feels calm rather than modal-heavy | HORIZON-02 | Visual rhythm and anxiety level are subjective | Open the target trigger in GraphPanel and confirm it feels like a subtle workspace tool, not a dashboard wizard |

## Human Checkpoint

1. Open GraphPanel and set a learning target.
2. Confirm the returned path renders as ghost nodes only, with no persistent graph mutation.
3. Click a ghost node and confirm the left panel switches into briefing mode with the generated definition.
4. Click `Start Learning (Crystallize)` and confirm a fresh chat opens with the selected concept seeded as the target.
5. Refresh the page and confirm the ghost path is ephemeral rather than silently persisted.
